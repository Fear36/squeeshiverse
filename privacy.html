Privacy Policy

SqueeshiVerse — last updated: November 25, 2026

SqueeshiVerse is a trading card game. This page explains what data the game uses, why, and how to delete it. We only collect what the game needs to work: no ads, no tracking, no selling of data.

Who is responsible?

The publisher of the game: Guillaume Trizac, reachable at dark-design@live.fr.

Data we use

  • Email address and password: to create your account and sign in. Your password is stored encrypted; we cannot read it.
  • Player ID: a random number that links your account to your collection.
  • Game data: cards collected, boosters opened, sparkles, goals completed, trades with other players, friend code, friends list and referred friends.
  • Crash reports: if the app crashes, iOS sends us an anonymous technical report (app and iOS version, error details) so we can fix the problem. It is not linked to any account.
  • Purchases: the pack you bought and the transaction ID provided by Apple, to credit your boosters. Payment itself is handled by Apple: we never see your payment details.

Other players only see a nickname like “Player 3F2A” and the cards you offer for trade. Your email address is never shown.

When you sign up, the app asks for your year of birth to check your age. It stays on your phone: it is never sent to or stored on our servers.

The daily reminder (“Your boosters are waiting!”) is scheduled on your phone, without going through our servers; you can turn it off in iOS Settings.

Where is the data stored?

On the servers of our host Nhost, located in Frankfurt (European Union). Purchases go through Apple, under its own privacy policy.

How long?

As long as your account exists. When you delete it, all your game data is erased immediately.

Deleting your account

In the app: profile button (top left of the collection) → Delete my account. Deletion is permanent. You can also email us at the address above.

Children and teenagers

The game is also meant for young players. In France, a minor under 15 needs a parent's agreement to create an account: the app asks for it at sign-up. In the United States, sign-up is limited to players aged 13 and over. Purchases and links outside the app are protected by a parental gate, in addition to Apple's “Screen Time” and “Ask to Buy” settings.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can access, correct or delete your data, object to its use or request a copy, by emailing dark-design@live.fr. You can also contact the French data protection authority, the CNIL (cnil.fr).

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page will be updated and the change will be announced in the game.
