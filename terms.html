SqueeshiVerse — last updated: November 25, 2026

The game

SqueeshiVerse is published by Guillaume Trizac (dark-design@live.fr). By creating an account, you accept these terms. If you are under 15, a parent must agree. In the United States, you must be at least 13.

Boosters and cards

  • Every player receives 3 free boosters per day (reset at midnight, Paris time).
  • Cards are drawn at random by our servers. The odds for each rarity are shown below.
  • Cards and boosters are virtual items: they have no real-money value, cannot be resold and remain tied to your account.

Draw odds

A booster contains 5 cards: 3 Common, 1 Rare, then a 5th card which is:

  • Rare: 70%
  • Epic: 25%
  • Legendary: 4.9%
  • Ultimate: 0.1% (1 booster in 1,000)

Purchases

Extra boosters can be bought in the app. Payment is handled by Apple; refund requests go through Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com). Purchased boosters that haven't been opened are lost if you delete your account.

Sparkles and goals

  • A duplicate can be recycled into sparkles: 5 (Common), 20 (Rare), 80 (Epic), 400 (Legendary), 1,000 (Ultimate).
  • Sparkles let you craft a missing card: 50 (Common), 200 (Rare), 800 (Epic), 3,200 (Legendary). The Ultimate card cannot be crafted.
  • Completing a card family gives 5 boosters (once per family); each daily challenge gives 30 sparkles.
  • Sparkles cannot be bought and have no real-money value.

Trades, friends and referrals

  • Trades are one card for one card, duplicates only, with any player or with your friends (added with their friend code).
  • Referrals give 1 booster to the referrer and 1 to the new player, up to 10 friends per referrer. Creating fake accounts to earn boosters is not allowed.

Fair play

Any cheating (modifying the app, exploiting a bug, multiple accounts) may lead to the account being deleted, without refund of the boosters involved.

Availability

We do our best to keep the game running at all times, but cannot guarantee it. The game may change over time (new cards, new rules).
